# Deepa Steel logo exports

- **Deepa-Steel-DS-Square.png**: 1200 × 1200 PNG. Use this for square ad-logo upload fields.
- **Deepa-Steel-Full-Logo.png**: 1200 × 300 PNG. Includes the DS mark, DEEPA STEEL and IRON & STEEL SUPPLIERS; use where a horizontal logo is accepted.

These are browser-rendered exports of the existing website typography and colours: gold #a98740, dark green #26332d, cream background #f6f5f0. The PNGs have an opaque cream background. They are not a newly designed logo or a claim of trademark registration.

Open the image, right-click (two-finger tap on Chromebook), and choose Save image as. Save as PNG, then choose it in the campaign's Logo / Upload image field. Exact ad-tool labels vary.
